Method

How DriftAI measures mispricing in tokenized equities — the full methodology.

The two markets

Each Stock Token on Robinhood Chain is a tokenized equity — a claim on real shares held by a licensed custodian — trading continuously against USDG on automated market maker (AMM) pools. The underlying equity trades on its primary venue (Nasdaq/NYSE) only during defined sessions: pre-market, regular hours, and after-hours. Two venues, one asset, radically different liquidity and hours.

Drift, defined

Drift is the token’s basis to its underlying: (P token − P ref) / P ref, expressed in percent. Positive drift is a premium, negative a discount. P token is the on-chain spot price of the deepest USDG pool; P ref is the freshest primary-market print available, including extended sessions. Both are sampled every 60 seconds, around the clock.

Why drift exists

During primary sessions, arbitrageurs keep the two venues in line — any gap is a riskless trade against custodial redemption, so it closes fast. Outside those sessions the tether goes slack: the token becomes the only live market for the equity, and its price is set purely by on-chain order flow in shallow pools. Small flows move thin liquidity a long way. That is where dislocations build.

Liquidity classification

Every reading is tagged by pool activity over the trailing hour. LIVE: five or more swaps — the quote reflects active two-way flow. THIN: at least one swap — directionally informative, sizing unreliable. STALE: zero swaps — the “price” is the last trade, potentially hours old; apparent drift on a stale pool is usually the reference moving, not the token. Stale quotes are never treated as signal.

Two kinds of dislocation

A gap can be noise or information. Flow-driven dislocation — thin-pool order flow with no news behind it — mean-reverts at the next open as arbitrage re-engages; this is DriftAI’s core convergence signal. Information-driven repricing — the token reacting to news released while the primary market is shut — is the token leading price discovery, and is expected to persist. Separating the two is the analytical heart of the system: volume context, news flow, and the direction of adjacent tickers all feed the classification.

Call protocol

Calls are issued only on LIVE readings that clear a minimum dislocation threshold, and only for convergence toward the underlying — never predictions about the equity itself. Each call is timestamped before the outcome is knowable and graded against the first hour of the next primary session. Results, including misses, are published on the scorecard. No cherry-picking, no deleted calls.

Known limitations

Honest caveats: the reference feed can lag the tape by minutes, so intraday drift under ~0.3% is within measurement noise. Quoted AMM spot is a mid, not an executable price — real fills pay slippage and gas, so small gaps aren’t tradeable edge. Corporate actions (dividends, splits) apply via token multipliers and can briefly distort raw comparisons. Coverage is limited to pools meeting a minimum daily volume floor.

What DriftAI is not

Not an oracle feed, not an execution system, and not investment advice. DriftAI is a measurement and publication layer: it observes the basis between two venues, classifies it, and reports it with full auditability. What anyone does with that information is their own decision and risk.

DriftAI provides informational market observations only. Nothing here is financial, investment, or trading advice. Tokenized equities and on-chain markets carry significant risk. Do your own research.
